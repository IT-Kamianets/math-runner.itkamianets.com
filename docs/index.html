Run to the right answer.

A 3D street runner where the lane you run into is your answer.

Download for Windows How it plays

Free to play · Windows 10 & 11

  • 3lanes to choose
  • 4levels of math
  • 3lives per run

One question, three lanes

The runner moves forward on its own. You only decide where to stand.

  1. 1

    Read the question

    It floats above the track while you run toward the next gate.

  2. 2

    Run into your answer

    Each lane holds one answer. There is nothing to tap: the lane you are in at the boards is your choice.

  3. 3

    Keep your streak

    Every 5 right answers in a row add a bonus. A wrong one costs a life.

Score and lives

You start with 3 lives. The run ends when they are gone.

3 lives per run

Correct

+10 points, plus 1 for every 5 correct in a row.

Wrong

Lose a life and your streak resets.

Obstacle

Lose a life. Switch lanes, jump or slide to avoid it.

Coin

+1 point each. Power-ups: slow motion, magnet, shield and an extra life.

Four levels of math

Wrong answers sit close to the right one, so guessing does not work.

LevelOperationsNumbers
Easy+ and −1 to 20
Medium+ and −5 to 50
Hard+ − ×2 to 50, tables to 10
Expert+ − × ÷5 to 99, tables to 12
